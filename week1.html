Creative Coding Project: The Wild Awakening IX by R Gopakumar

What speaks to me about R. Gopahumars Wild Awakening is how he brings together two very contrasting aspects of todays world i.e., nature and technology, in a rather harmonious way. His work makes the scenery come alive making it almost real. Gopakumar takes us inside seemingly a forest, making one wonder about the very much prevalent issue of climate change. It highlights what were at risk of losing and could be a call to action. The artist uses coding not just as a technical tool that it is normally thought to be, but rather as a form of art and for social commentary. I resonate with this because that's how I would also want to use coding as I learn it. Thats what I think creative coding means to me - being able to tell stories through visual languages. landing page
